What is Geeks Hub

Geeks Hub is elite cybersecurity expertise — the latest zero-days, breaches, hacks and tech news, decoded by elite experts, second by second. If it is being exploited, leaked, patched or weaponized anywhere on Earth, it passes through here first.

Every day we ship short, sharp videos on CVEs, tips & tricks, and the biggest stories in cyber security and A.I. — researched from primary sources, stripped of hype, and cut for people who actually live in this world.

Made for geeks everywhere — defenders, researchers, the curious, and everyone who wants to see the attack coming before it lands.
