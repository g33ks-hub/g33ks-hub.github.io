Privacy Policy

Geeks Hub publishes short technology and cybersecurity videos on Facebook and YouTube, and is expanding to TikTok. This policy explains what information is involved when you watch, follow, comment on, or message us — and how we use the platform APIs that publish our own videos to our own accounts.

Effective: November 6, 2026 Last updated: November 6, 2026 Applies to: Geeks Hub on Facebook, YouTube & TikTok

At a glance

  • We publish our own content. Our software and API access are used only to post Geeks Hub videos to Geeks Hub accounts.
  • We do not sell personal data — and we do not buy it, rent it, or trade it with data brokers.
  • No accounts, forms, or sign-ups here. This page collects nothing from you when you read it.
  • Your interactions live on the platforms. Comments, likes, and messages are handled by Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok under their own privacy policies.

01Who we are

Geeks Hub is a technology media project that produces short-form videos about cybersecurity, vulnerabilities (CVEs), hacks, tips, tricks, and artificial intelligence news. Geeks Hub operates a Facebook Page named “Geeks Hub”, a YouTube channel at youtube.com/@g33ks-hub, and is bringing the same brand to TikTok. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean the Geeks Hub project and the people who run it.

02What this policy covers

This policy covers the Geeks Hub pages, channels, and accounts listed above, this privacy policy page itself, and the publishing tools and platform APIs we use to post our videos. It does not govern Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, or Google themselves: when you use those services, your information is also handled under each platform’s own privacy policy and terms, which we encourage you to read.

03Information we receive

Geeks Hub is a content publisher, not a data service. The information that reaches us falls into four narrow categories:

Information you choose to share publicly or with us

  • Public interactions — your name or handle, profile picture, comments, reactions, and shares on our posts and videos. These are visible to us and, depending on your platform settings, to other viewers.
  • Messages — if you send us a direct message through one of our pages or channels, we receive the content of that message and the account you sent it from.

Aggregated insights from the platforms

Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok provide us with audience analytics about our own content — for example view counts, watch time, and aggregated audience demographics such as country-level location and age ranges. These insights are aggregated statistics provided by the platforms; they do not identify individual viewers to us.

Access credentials for our own accounts

To publish automatically, we hold access tokens and API credentials that belong to Geeks Hub’s own accounts only. These credentials let our publishing tools upload videos, set titles and captions, and read our own content’s performance. They do not give us access to your account, your videos, or your personal data, and we never ask viewers for their passwords or credentials.

This page

This privacy policy page is a static document. It has no accounts, no forms, no comment system, and sets no tracking cookies. We do not run analytics on it.

04How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Publish, schedule, and manage Geeks Hub videos on our own accounts;
  • Respond to comments and messages you send us;
  • Understand which topics our audience finds useful, so we can plan future episodes;
  • Keep our accounts and publishing tools secure and working correctly; and
  • Comply with the rules of the platforms we publish on and with applicable law.

We do not use information to build profiles of individual viewers, to serve targeted advertising of our own, or for automated decision-making about you.

05How we use platform APIs

Geeks Hub uses official developer APIs to publish its own videos to its own accounts — the Meta integration below is in use today, and the YouTube and TikTok integrations are being connected under this same policy. Our use of each API is limited to that purpose:

YouTube API Services (Google)

We use YouTube API Services to upload Geeks Hub videos to the Geeks Hub YouTube channel and to manage that channel’s own content (titles, descriptions, and publishing status). Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • API access is used solely to publish and manage our own channel’s content;
  • We do not collect, request, or store personal data of YouTube viewers through the API;
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising;
  • Access tokens are stored securely, are accessible only to the people who operate Geeks Hub, and are revoked when no longer needed.

By using our YouTube channel you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta / Facebook

We use Meta’s developer platform and Graph API to publish Reels and posts to the Geeks Hub Facebook Page and to read that Page’s own insights. We access only data belonging to the Geeks Hub Page, in accordance with Meta’s Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

TikTok

Subject to TikTok’s review and approval of our developer app, we use TikTok’s Content Posting API to post Geeks Hub videos to the Geeks Hub TikTok account. We access only our own account’s data, in accordance with TikTok’s Developer Terms of Service and the permissions TikTok grants our app.

The short version: every API we use points at our own accounts and our own videos. None of them is used to access, collect, or process information about our viewers.

06Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is shared only in these limited situations:

  • With the platforms themselves — publishing a video necessarily sends that video and its caption to Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok, which process it under their own policies;
  • Publicly, by design — comments and reactions you post on our content are public on the platform where you posted them;
  • For legal reasons — if we are required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of Geeks Hub or others, we may disclose information we hold.

07Retention & security

Comments and messages remain on the platforms where you posted them, under those platforms’ retention rules, until you delete them or the content is removed. We keep API credentials and publishing records only for as long as they are needed to operate Geeks Hub, store them in access-controlled credential storage rather than in plain documents or chats, and revoke or rotate them when they are no longer required.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the credentials and information under our control against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

08Your choices & rights

  • Control your interactions: you can edit or delete your comments, and delete messages you sent us, at any time inside Facebook, YouTube, or TikTok.
  • Unfollow or block: you can unfollow, unsubscribe from, or block Geeks Hub on any platform at any time.
  • Ask us: you may contact us (see Section 11) to ask what information of yours we hold outside the platforms — in practice this is limited to messages you sent us — and to request its deletion. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Platform settings: your broader privacy rights over data held by Google, Meta, and TikTok — including access, correction, and deletion — are exercised through those companies’ privacy settings and policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. The commitments above — no sale of personal data, no viewer profiling, and deletion on request of anything we ourselves hold — apply to every viewer regardless of location.

09Children’s privacy

Geeks Hub content is made for a general adult audience interested in technology and cybersecurity. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the platforms’ requirements change. The current version will always be posted at this page, with the “Last updated” date at the top revised accordingly. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

11Contact us

You can also reach the channel on YouTube: youtube.com/@g33ks-hub

Please never send us passwords, API keys, or other credentials — Geeks Hub will never ask for them.
